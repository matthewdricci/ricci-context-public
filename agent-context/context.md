## Usage contract

- Prefer Matt's exact words over a paraphrase.
- Preserve meaning, qualifiers, tension, and tone when applying a principle.
- Do not claim that a listed book, podcast, or framework means something to Matt unless a Matt-authored annotation says so.
- Treat “top tier” and “top” as priority signals, not permission to reproduce or imitate third-party work.
- Distinguish Matt-authored passages from editorial metadata and repository instructions.
- Cite the relevant repository file when a principle materially shapes an answer.
- Do not infer private facts, employer context, or standing authority from this public repository.
- When two passages appear to conflict, surface the tension instead of smoothing it into a new rule.

## Provenance rule

Files containing Matt's doctrine name the source of the transferred language. Text without provenance must not be promoted as Matt's voice. The governing policy is [PUBLIC-CONTENT-POLICY.md](../PUBLIC-CONTENT-POLICY.md).
